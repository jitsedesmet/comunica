# Acknowledgements

## Async and pre-order algebra traversal utilities

The asynchronous and pre-order variants of the algebra traversal helpers in
[`@comunica/utils-algebra`](packages/utils-algebra) (`mapOperationAsync`, `mapOperationPreOrder`,
`mapOperationPreOrderAsync`, their `Sub` and `Strict` counterparts, and `visitOperationAsync` /
`visitOperationSubAsync`), together with the migration of existing Comunica actors onto them,
were developed by Jitse De Smet as part of his PhD research at
[IDLab](https://idlab.technology/), Ghent University – imec.
They build on the transformer infrastructure of [TRAQULA](https://github.com/comunica/traqula).
